# F2P Ironman Quest Route

All 24 free-to-play Old School RuneScape quests as one walking route for ironmen.

Instead of doing each quest from start to finish, the route mixes steps from different quests by location, so you pick up items on the way instead of running back and forth. It starts in Lumbridge right after Learning the Ropes.

**Open the route:** https://bengladine.github.io/osrs-quest-route

## What's in it

- One ordered checklist across every F2P quest, in 10 sections
- Mark quests you've already done and their steps are crossed out
- Fill in your combat and skill levels to get warnings at steps you're not ready for
- Quest points counted automatically for quest requirements
- An inventory check per section: what to carry and what can stay in the bank
- The dialogue options you need, and links to the OSRS Wiki for maps and pictures
- Your progress is saved in your browser

## Found a mistake?

Open an issue with the "Route mistake" template. Mention the section and step, what the guide says, and what actually happened in game.
