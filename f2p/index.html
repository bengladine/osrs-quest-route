F2P Ironman Quest Route

All 25 free-to-play quests as one walking route, starting in Lumbridge. Steps from different quests are mixed by location, so you pick things up on the way instead of running back and forth. Tick steps off as you go; everything is saved in this browser.

Quests: doing, done or skip

Done: its steps are crossed out. Skip: its steps disappear, for quests you don't meet the requirements for or don't want to do. Steps that serve several quests stay until none of those quests is still on Doing.

Coins: you'll need a few hundred coins over the route (boats, tolls, dyes, an iron chainbody). Pirate's Treasure pays 450 back.

Imps: kill every imp you see along the way. You need 1 red, 1 yellow, 1 black and 1 white bead for Imp Catcher. Short on one or two: Falador's south entrance. Grinding them all from scratch? The top of the Karamja volcano is better, with lots of imps close together, if you can spare the 30 coins for the boat from Port Sarim.

Armour and run energy: weight doesn't slow you down, but it drains run energy faster. Travel light through towns and put armour on for the fights.

Dialogue: where you must pick a specific chat option, it's in the step or under "Detailed steps". If an NPC doesn't mention your quest, look for "Talk about something else".
