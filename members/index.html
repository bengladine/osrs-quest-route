Members Ironman Quest Route

The members quests for ironmen, in chapters. Members and free-to-play quests are mixed by location, so you pick things up on the way and every quest unlocks the next. Progress is shared with the F2P route: a quest done there is crossed out here too.

Quests: doing, done or skip

Done: its steps are crossed out. Skip: its steps disappear, for quests you don't meet the requirements for or don't want to do. Steps that serve several quests stay until none of those quests is still on Doing.

Order: the whole route follows the OSRS Wiki's optimal ironman quest order, from the tutorial to the quest point cape, in 10 chapters. Some quests start in one chapter and finish in a later one; those steps say so. Finished sections fold away by themselves.

New: chapter 1 has been played through. Chapters 2 to 10 are new and not playtested yet, and from chapter 4 on the steps get shorter. If a step doesn't match the game, the quest tag opens its full wiki guide, and a mistake report helps fix it.

Lamps: where a step says where to spend an XP lamp, that's to reach the levels the next quests need.

Coins: the Stronghold of Security pays 10,000 coins early on. You'll need most of it for the Lumber Yard and travel.

Armour and run energy: weight doesn't slow you down, but it drains run energy faster. Travel light through towns and put armour on for the fights.

Dialogue: where you must pick a specific chat option, it's in the step or under "Detailed steps". If an NPC doesn't mention your quest, look for "Talk about something else".
