OSRS Ironman Quest Routes

Quest guides usually go one quest at a time, so you run back and forth for items. These routes mix the steps of many quests by location: you pick things up on the way and do everything nearby before moving on.

F2P route

All 25 free-to-play quests, plus Dragon Slayer I and Shield of Arrav, from Lumbridge onwards.

Members route

Members and F2P quests in the ironman order, in chapters. Chapter 1 (about 20 quests) is ready; more chapters follow.

Open the members route →

How it works

  1. Type your RuneScape name and load your account. Your levels come from Wise Old Man; your quests from WikiSync if you use that RuneLite plugin.
  2. Quests you've done are crossed out. Steps you're not ready for turn orange.
  3. Tick steps as you go. Your progress stays in your browser and is shared between both routes.
