F2P Ironman Quest Route

All 24 free-to-play quests as one walking route, starting in Lumbridge. Steps from different quests are mixed by location, so you pick things up on the way instead of running back and forth. Tick steps off as you go; everything is saved in this browser.

Coins: you'll need a few hundred coins over the route (boats, tolls, dyes, an iron chainbody). Pirate's Treasure pays 450 back.

Imps: kill every imp you see along the way. You need 1 red, 1 yellow, 1 black and 1 white bead for Imp Catcher. Best spot if you're short: Falador's south entrance.

Armour and run energy: weight doesn't slow you down, but it drains run energy faster. Travel light through towns and put armour on for the fights.

Dialogue: where you must pick a specific chat option, it's in the step or under "Detailed steps". If an NPC doesn't mention your quest, look for "Talk about something else".
